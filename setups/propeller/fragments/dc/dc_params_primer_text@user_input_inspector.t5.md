A fixed reference supplied with your instructions. It is NOT a user input: never
describe it back to the user, and never count it among the user's uploaded images.
The diagram carries parameter labels; you do not need them - record what the USER

LEFT - top view, looking along the rotation axis.
  - hub            = the central cylinder, radius 8 mm.
  - inner section  = blade root, at r = 4 mm.  This is NOT the hub radius:
    the inner section sits INSIDE the hub, and r = 4 mm is where the blade
    span starts.  The enlarged corner of the diagram dimensions both.
  - middle section = anywhere in the green band.  Its position is a fraction of
    the BLADE SPAN measured from the 4 mm root, not of the overall radius.
  - outer section  = blade tip, at the outer radius.
  - Diameter is measured on the ring MID-WALL (orange dashes), not on either
    wall face.
  - One blade is drawn; a propeller has several.

RIGHT - nine blade sections, trailing edge LEFT and leading edge RIGHT (the same
orientation the blade-section renders use). All nine share one chord and one
thickness; only camber and high-point change.
  - camber, in % of chord, increases left to right.
  - the high point of the camber line, in tenths of chord, moves top to bottom.
  - thickness is a % of that section's OWN chord - how thick it is, not where it
    is thickest.
  - angle of attack is measured from the horizontal plane.  The sections are
    drawn chord-horizontal so their shapes compare directly; the centre cell
    shows the angle separately.
  - The middle section's profile follows from the inner and outer ones.
