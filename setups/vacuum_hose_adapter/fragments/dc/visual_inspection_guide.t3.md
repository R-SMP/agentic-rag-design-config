A vacuum hose adapter with correct geometry should show:
- Two ends (connectors) joined by a continuous transition, open all the
  way through — a hollow duct with no wall closing it.
- Each end in the style asked for: a plain or barbed hose end, a flange, a
  magnetic flange with its magnet holes, a nozzle.
- A bend matching the requested angle, when one was asked for.
- Smooth surfaces without holes, spikes, or self-intersections.
- Proportions consistent with the input parameters (the two ends'
  diameters and lengths, the transition's length).

### What you can typically check visually for this DC

  * Which end is the larger one, and roughly by how much.
  * The bend angle (side view): straight, 45°, 90°.
  * Each end's style: hose end, flange, magnetic flange (magnet holes
    visible), nozzle.
  * A stop ring or barbs on a hose end; screw holes on a flange.
  * Whether the second end is repeated (a splitter).

### What is typically NOT resolvable at render resolution

  * Walls and tapers of a few tenths of a millimetre.
  * Exact diameters within ~1–2 mm: read them from the parameters, not the
    picture.
  * Thread and grid details.

When a claim falls in the "not resolvable" bucket, mark it as
such and trust falls on the DE's parameter choice.
