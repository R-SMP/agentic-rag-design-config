The vacuum hose adapter consists of:
1. Connector 1 (end 1): its style (a hose connector, a magnetic flange, a flat
   flange, an osVAC fitting, or a ready-made fitting for a named system), its
   diameter — the end's inside or outside, see ``End1_Measurement`` — its
   length, wall thickness and taper, with an optional straight extension and
   a stop ring.
2. A transition joining the two ends: a straight taper, a bend, a curved
   bend, a flat plate or a hull, with its own length, angle and bend radius.
3. Connector 2 (end 2): the same kinds of settings as end 1, and also a nozzle
   or no end at all; it can be repeated (``Transition_End2_Count``) to make a
   splitter.
4. Connector 3: used only as the centre port of a ``hull`` transition
   (``Transition_HullCenter = end3``).
