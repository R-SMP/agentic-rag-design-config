**Name the diameter: inside or outside.**

Whenever you ask for a diameter change — or ask for one to be HELD — say
which diameter you mean:
  * outside — "make end 1's outside diameter about 2 mm smaller so it slides
    into the hose"
  * inside — "keep end 2's inside diameter at 100 mm so it goes over the port"
  * held — "keep both ends' diameters as they are; change only the bend"
