The vacuum hose adapter consists of two ends (connectors) joined by a
transition:
1. Connector 1 and connector 2: each a hose connector, a flange, a magnetic
   flange, an osVAC fitting or a ready-made fitting for a named system (end 2
   also a nozzle), with a diameter, a length and a wall.
2. The transition: a taper, a bend or a flat plate between them.
